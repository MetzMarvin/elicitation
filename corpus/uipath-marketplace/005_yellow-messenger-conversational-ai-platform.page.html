    Yellow Messenger Conversational AI Platform

    by Ijaaz Ahamed U N

    ★★★★★ 1 | Connector | <100
    Overview Reviews Q&A

    Summary

    Get invoices approved with Yellow Messenger's chatbot. Upload the scanned document to the chatbot, it captures & stores invoice data in the database and provides accurate information to the users.

    Overview

    Deploy your virtual assistant/chatbot for internal and external-facing users seamlessly across channels like Microsoft Teams, Slack, WhatsApp, etc. 

    This seamless integration with UiPath will allow users to get their invoices approved without going through any pain of filling out a form by manually seeing and typing out the information from the invoice and then getting their invoices approved.

    You can also do sentiment analysis for multiple reviews or feedback that you have received by simply uploading a CSV file containing reviews or feedback and the rest is taken care of by UiPath and our NLP engine.

    The pre-built integration takes the journey from the virtual assistant/Chatbot to the UiPath Orchestrator and then back to the Chatbot, thereby completing a full circle to give a seamless experience to the users.

    Features

    1. Enhanced User Experience 2. 2-way communication between UiPath and Chatbot 3. Faster processing of Invoice Approval 4. Insights on your Customer’s or Employee’s Sentiment 5. Omni-channel capability - Slack, Microsoft Teams, WhatsApp, etc. 6. 100+ Supported Languages

    Additional Information

    Dependencies
